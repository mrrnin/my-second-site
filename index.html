Where the four rivers of learning meet.

Chaktomuk High School is a bilingual Khmer-English school in Phnom Penh for grades 7–12, built around the belief that a strong education, like the city it stands in, draws its strength from many currents flowing into one.

Why families choose Chaktomuk

Rigorous, bilingual, and rooted in Phnom Penh.

Every student studies in both Khmer and English from Grade 7 onward, then chooses a pathway in Grade 10 that matches where they want to go next — university, technical study, or the workforce.

Curriculum

Bilingual by design

Core subjects are taught in Khmer with an English-medium track running alongside, so students graduate fluent in both academic registers.

Pathways

Three routes from Grade 10

STEM, Humanities & Social Science, and Applied Arts & Design — each with its own labs, electives, and capstone project.

Community

A campus that stays open

Library and computer labs run until 6pm on weekdays, and Saturday clubs keep the campus active outside the regular timetable.

“We didn't want a school that taught two languages side by side. We wanted one where Khmer and English think together — in the same lab report, the same debate, the same exam.”

— Principal, Chaktomuk High School
