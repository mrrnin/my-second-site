មុខវិជ្ជា — academics

Two years shared. Three ways forward.

Every student follows the same core curriculum in Grades 7–9. From Grade 10, they choose one of three pathways that shapes their timetable, electives, and final-year capstone project.

Grades 7–9

The shared core.

Before choosing a pathway, every student builds the same foundation — so the choice in Grade 10 is informed, not guessed at.

Grades 10–12

Choose a pathway.

Pathway 01

STEM

Advanced mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, and computer science, with a dedicated lab block four afternoons a week.

  • Applied Mathematics & Statistics
  • Computer Science & Robotics
  • Lab-based Physics and Chemistry
  • Capstone: independent research project
Pathway 02

Humanities & Social Science

Focused study of history, economics, geography, and comparative literature, with an emphasis on research and argument.

  • Khmer & World History
  • Economics & Development Studies
  • Comparative Literature
  • Capstone: extended research essay
Pathway 03

Applied Arts & Design

Visual art, design, and media production, taught alongside a small-business and portfolio-development track.

  • Visual Art & Design Fundamentals
  • Media Production
  • Portfolio & Exhibition Practice
  • Capstone: public exhibition

Beyond the timetable

Clubs, teams, and competitions.

  • Robotics & coding club — weekly, competes nationally.
  • Debate society — Khmer and English divisions.
  • Student newspaper — published each term.
  • Traditional and contemporary dance — open to all grades.
  • Football, volleyball & badminton teams — inter-school league.
  • Environmental club — river-cleanup partnership with local NGOs.
  • Model United Nations — regional conference each March.
  • Art & design collective — runs the annual exhibition.
