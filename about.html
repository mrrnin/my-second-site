អំពីសាលា — about the school

Built where four currents meet.

Chaktomuk — "four faces" — is the old name for the point in Phnom Penh where the Mekong, Bassac, and Tonlé Sap rivers converge. We took the name in 1998 because it says plainly what we believe a school should be.

Our history

From one borrowed classroom to a full campus.

Chaktomuk High School opened in 1998 with 34 students in a single borrowed classroom near the riverfront, teaching an evening programme for students who worked during the day. By 2006 the school had its own campus in Chroy Changvar; by 2015 it had grown into a full Grade 7–12 day school with dedicated science and computer labs.

The evening-school roots still shape how the school thinks about students: education here is treated as something built around a person's whole life, not the other way around.

Mission & vision

What we're trying to do.

Mission — to graduate students who can think rigorously and communicate confidently in both Khmer and English, and who leave prepared for university, skilled work, or the path they choose next.

Vision — a school where academic pathways are chosen by interest and ability rather than assumed by family background, and where the campus stays a genuine part of its neighbourhood.

What we hold to

Five things that don't change from year to year.

  • Bilingual by default. Khmer and English are both instructional languages, not "language class" and "everything else."
  • Small enough to notice. Class sizes are capped at 32 so teachers can track individual progress, not just averages.
  • Choice comes with structure. Pathways start in Grade 10, after two years of a shared core curriculum.
  • Assessment is more than exams. Every pathway includes a capstone project graded alongside written exams.
  • The campus serves the neighbourhood. Library and sports facilities open to the local community on weekends.
