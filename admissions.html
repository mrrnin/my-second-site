ការចូលរៀន — admissions

Four steps, one entry exam.

We admit new students into Grade 7 and Grade 10 each January, with a small number of mid-year places when space allows. Here's exactly how the process works.

The process

How admission works.

  1. 1

    Submit the application

    Complete the application form and upload the last two years of school reports. Applications for January entry open in July and close 20 September.

  2. 2

    Sit the entry exam

    A written exam in Khmer, English, and Mathematics, held on campus in early October. Results are released within two weeks.

  3. 3

    Family interview

    A short conversation with the Vice-Principal of Student Life — mainly to answer your questions and confirm the pathway of interest for Grade 10 applicants.

  4. 4

    Confirm your place

    Offers go out in November. A signed enrolment form and deposit secure the place before the school year begins in January.

What you'll need

Requirements & key dates.

Documents

  • Birth certificate or family book (copy)
  • School reports from the last two years
  • Two passport-size photos
  • Immunization record

Key dates — January intake

  • Applications open: 1 July
  • Applications close: 20 September
  • Entry exam: 4–5 October
  • Offers released: 10 November
  • Term begins: 5 January
